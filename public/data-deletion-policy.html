Data Deletion Policy

To comply with Google Play Store policies and Facebook (Meta) Platform Developer Rules (Section 3 - Data Deletion Request Callback), Soko Nairobi provides transparent, swift tools to request the deletion of all personal data, listings, chat logs, and coordinate bindings.

Method 1: Direct One-Click In-App Purge (Recommended)

The easiest way to invoke the "Right to be Forgotten" is directly through the Soko Nairobi portal. This triggers an automated, instant cascading deletion inside our PostgreSQL database:

Instructions:

  1. Log in to your Soko Nairobi account using your credentials, Google Account, or Facebook Login.
  2. Open the bottom navigation menu and click on the Profile Tab.
  3. Scroll to the bottom of your Profile card where you will find the red "Delete Soko Account & Purge Data" button.
  4. Confirm your selection in the warning modal.

⚠️ Warning: This action is 100% permanent and irreversible. Your active listings, rating history, chat negotiations, and system notifications will be instantly deleted.

Method 2: Meta (Facebook) Data Deletion Callback URL

If you linked Soko Nairobi via Facebook Login and wish to request data deletion via Meta's automated callback engine:

  1. Go to your personal Facebook Account's Settings & Privacy > Settings.
  2. In the left sidebar, click Apps and Websites.
  3. Search for "Soko Nairobi" and click Remove.
  4. In the prompt, select the option to send a deletion request. This will dispatch a signed webhook request to our Meta callback endpoint.
  5. Our endpoint immediately logs the deletion command and returns a unique confirmation code. You can verify your status anytime.

Method 3: Manual Email Data Removal Request

If you are unable to access the application dashboard or require administrator assistance:

  • Send an email request to our lead developer at ioproxxy@gmail.com.
  • Subject: Soko Nairobi Data Deletion Request - [Your Username]
  • Please provide either your registered email address, username, or your Safaricom mobile phone number to allow verification.
  • Our administrative team will review, manually execute the delete queries, and reply with a written confirmation of erasure within 24 hours.

4. What Data is Cleared on Deletion?

When a deletion is executed (either via in-app wipe, Meta webhook, or email request), the following data is permanently purged:

❌ Identity Parameters Full Name, Email Address, Avatar URL, and Password hashes are deleted.
❌ Classified Listings All active, pending, or sold product listings are completely unlinked and deleted.
❌ Chat Messages All sent and received peer-to-peer message histories are erased.
❌ Location Coordinates All ward selections, precise calibration latitude/longitude settings are purged.
