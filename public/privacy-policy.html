Privacy Policy

Soko Nairobi ("we", "our", or "us") operates the local proximity trader platform Sokos.co.ke (accessible at https://sokos.co.ke). We are fully committed to protecting your privacy and personal identifiers. This document outlines how we collect, manage, and delete data, specifically tailored to satisfy standard Meta (Facebook) and Google OAuth verification requirements.

1. OAuth Identity Authentication & Data Collected

To enable safe and fast registration without password overhead, we support single-sign-on (SSO) authentication via Google Accounts and Meta (Facebook) Login. When you link your Soko Nairobi account with these providers, we request access to your public profile parameters. We collect and store:

  • Your Legal Name: Used to display on your vendor cards, review panels, and buyer-seller trust handshakes.
  • Your Email Address: Used as a secure contact channel, primary account identification, and compliance notifications.
  • Your Profile Avatar Image: Used as your trade session profile picture to establish friendly identity.
  • Provider Unique Social ID: Stored securely to manage the authentication handshakes on subsequent visits.

2. Proximity Coordinates & Privacy Safeguards

Soko Nairobi is a localized marketplace matching traders within a 5 kilometer coordinate threshold. Our platform requests your location coordinates (Latitude and Longitude) based on Nairobi County Wards or your browser's GPS:

  • Coordinate Protection: Your exact GPS coordinates are NEVER shown to other users. We inject a slight random fuzzing algorithm (offsets of 100 to 150 meters) on all maps to ensure your physical residence is 100% hidden.
  • Proximity Computation: We only use the mathematical distance vectors on the server to prioritize sorting nearby listings first on user dashboards.

3. Safe Data Sharing Policy

Soko Nairobi is built with zero third-party tracking, profiling, or cookie monetization. We do not sell, rent, or lease any merchant information to advertisers. Your data is strictly shared with other users only in the following active trade scenarios:

  • Active Escrow Transactions: When a buyer initiates an order and selects M-Pesa STK Push payment, we disclose the seller's verified contact number to complete the mobile transfer.
  • Chat Messages: Communication text is stored securely and is only visible to the two active participants of the chat box.

4. Your Control: Instantly Revoking & Deleting Data

We believe in absolute data ownership. You have complete control to revoke, request, or delete your entire profile footprint from our servers instantly:

  • In-App One-Click Wipe: Navigate to your Profile Tab in the dashboard and click "Delete Account". This triggers a cascading SQL purge, deleting your user record, listings, chat history, notifications, and location coordinates within milliseconds.
  • External Provider Removal: You can revoke Soko's access anytime via your Google App permissions or Facebook Apps Settings page. Review our Data Deletion Policy for specific instructions.
